# Chapter 6: QCD for colliders (hadronization and jet quenching)

Model `estimates/ch06_collider.py`, tests `estimates/tests/test_ch06_collider.py`, paper chapter `applications/app10_collider_physics.tex` (Chapter 6, label `ch:collider`). Every number below is read from the model, from `resources.json` or from a test, and the place it comes from is named beside it.

## 1. What is estimated

The chapter asks whether a quantum computer can follow a quark-antiquark pair through hadronization in real time and count what comes out. The model prices five circuits, which are the five Ch. 6 rows of `resources.json`.

- **2028 benchmark: readout pipeline.** 2+1D lattice gauge theory with the Z3 subgroup of SU(3) (2 qubits per link) on a 4^2 lattice with three staggered fields (u, d, s-like), quench-injected source, one adiabatic ramp step and two Trotter steps of evolution. It is a test of the circuit and its computational-basis records against an emulation of the same circuit, not a physics result: one ramp step does not reach the interacting vacuum and two steps reach t = 0.2 a.
- **2033 target: hadron-species ratios, and the FCC-era fragmentation function.** 2+1D SU(3) digitized on Sigma(72x3) (9 qubits per link), a = 0.1 fm, 4x8 lattice (cut from 4x16), three staggered fields. A gauge-invariant q qbar wave packet is injected at the box momenta 1.55, 3.1 and 4.65 GeV, the vacuum is prepared by an adiabatic ramp, the pair evolves for 30-50 a (3-5 fm/c), and the same ramp is run backwards so that strong-coupling labels become hadron-species operators N_h = U Pi_h U^dag. One circuit is read out two ways: (a) direct sampling of species counts, giving the baryon-to-meson ratio B/M and kaon rates (a first result at 30% from one configuration, and a campaign that resolves the B/M trend across three source momenta at 3 sigma, plus a one-point box-size check at L_par = 4); (b) a Hadamard test of a bilocal current, giving the fragmentation function D_pi^q(z) in 10 z-bins (the FCC-era instance).
- **2033 first-result dipole rows.** Two pure-gauge circuits that hold a colour-singlet dipole of size r = a = 0.2 fm in a thermal medium at aT ~ 0.45 and measure its singlet survival P_s at 0.5 and 1 fm/c. The static row extracts Im V(r = a, T) on Sigma(216x3) with the improved Hamiltonian H_I on 3^3; the light-like row extracts q-hat on the binary octahedral group 2O (SU(2)) on 3x3x5. The medium is quench-prepared (electric vacuum, coupling ramp, then c / T of evolution under H) and thermalization is assumed (eigenstate thermalization / typicality).
- **Co-design row (not a box).** The 3+1D extension: Sigma(72x3) on 4^3 with three staggered fields, 10^3 Trotter steps, no ramp. It is the vehicle for full-QCD q-hat and is past the 2033 reference (filed under the era `codesign`).

## 2. How to run

From the repository root, with the packages of `requirements.txt` installed:

```
python -m estimates --chapter ch06                    # this chapter's report (read-only)
python -m estimates --chapter ch06 --intermediates    # plus every intermediate the model records
python -m pytest estimates/tests/test_ch06_collider.py -q
python applications/ch06_collider/dump_assumptions.py # the assumption table of section 3
```

The chapter report prints three blocks. ASSUMPTIONS lists the 129 fields of `Assumptions` with value, provenance and source. INSTANCES lists the five rows the chapter writes to `resources.json`. EXPORTS gives, per era, the headline logical qubits and per-shot T against the value the paper's box prints (marked `[ok]` when inside the box's rounding tolerance), shots, wall time, epsilon_l, the T-depth and factory keys, the primitive-by-primitive T breakdown with each primitive's circuit status, and the model's own notes on how the numbers combine.

The test file currently gives `66 passed, 357 skipped, 1 xfailed`. The skips are the tests that compare the model against the chapter's LaTeX text; they run only when the paper source sits beside the package. The one expected failure is a strict tripwire: it asserts that the Sigma(72x3) colour-frame count is a compiled circuit, which it is not yet (section 6), and it will start failing loudly the day the source compiles it.

## 4. How the numbers are built

All line numbers refer to `estimates/ch06_collider.py` unless another file is named.

### 4.1 Machinery shared by every circuit

**Register** (`register`, lines 676-681; eq. Nq_collider). Logical qubits are N_q = q_G V n_link + N_c N_stag V + N_anc, with q_G the compiled qubits per link from `groups.GROUPS[...].link_qubits` (`link_width`, 663-668), n_link = D links per site, and one Jordan-Wigner mode per colour per site per staggered field. Magic-state factories are not counted.

**Per-step T.** One Trotter step of H_KS costs `n_links x (magnetic + electric + hop) + V x mass`. The gauge terms come from the subgroup papers' gate tables in `groups.py` with the per-link multiplicities of `groups.PRIMCOST` (for KS in 2D: 3 U_inv, 6 U_mul, 1/2 U_Tr, 2 Fourier transforms and one U_phi per link). `gauge_step` (721-757) and `electric_floor_per_link` (709-718) evaluate them; `fourier_key` (702-706) picks the fast Fourier transform where one exists. The hop is `hop_link` (803-819, calling `groups.hop_link_cost`), the staggered mass `mass_site` (822-828, one Hamming-weight phasing group over the N_c N_stag = 9 copies per site). `gauge_primitives` (760-784) and `fermion_primitives` (831-858) turn these into the itemized breakdown that the chapter report prints.

**Rotation pricing** (`rtol_eps` 892-898, `_at_eps` 901-904, `synth_rotations_per_step` 861-867). Every synthesized rotation costs 1.15 log2(1/eps) + 9.2 T and every Toffoli 7 T. A primitive whose source quotes `a + b log2(1/eps)` is priced as `a` T plus `b / 1.15` rotations, each rotation at that full fit. The tolerance is set per circuit: the circuit counts its own rotations N_rot per shot (exact T gates and Toffolis are not rotations), and with randomized synthesis the errors add as N_rot eps^2, so eps = sqrt(eps_syn / N_rot) with eps_syn = 10^-2. A longer circuit therefore pays slightly more per rotation; each end of a ramp range and each dipole circuit is priced at its own eps.

**Hamming-weight phasing** (`common.hwp_group`). k equal-angle rotations on copies that share an angle cost floor(log2 k) + 1 rotations and k - w(k) Toffolis with measurement-based uncompute, holding k - w(k) ancilla. For k = 9 that is 4 rotations, 7 Toffolis and 7 ancilla.

**Fault budget.** The required logical error rate is epsilon_l = 0.1 / (T per shot), 0.1 expected faults per shot (`clean_shot_faults`).

**Wall time and T-depth.** One machine, serial: a shot takes max(N_T x 1 us, D_T x 10 us) + 0.1 ms (`wall_corrected`, 992-994; t_r = 10 us is `common.REACTION_TIME_S`). The 1 us per T is the report's ten-factory baseline; the D_T term applies only when the circuit is too sequential for ten factories (F* = N_T / D_T < 10). `common.depth_exports` returns the T-depth, F*, the depth floor and the factories needed for one year.

### 4.2 The 2028 benchmark (`_model_2028`, lines 1052-1378)

## 7. Open items and limitations

From `docs/OPEN_ITEMS.md` and the chapter's algorithm-development gap. None of these changes a printed number until it is decided.

*Readout and observables.*
- The species map N_h = U Pi_h U^dag uses strong-coupling labels and the preparation ramp run backwards. How label populations relate to hadron yields is not established, the backward ramp is not shown to be adiabatic far above the vacuum (its price is a lower bound), and the misidentification rate at Sigma(72x3) 4x8 has no estimate.
- Vector and pseudoscalar mesons are degenerate at strong coupling. A V/PS readout, and momentum-resolved N_h(P_h), need a transform over the label register that is neither constructed nor priced.
- The fragmentation shots are lower bounds: they omit N_h, whose per-bin variance raises them 4.7x to 23x (`ff_nh_shot_factor`). At L_par = 8 the momentum quantum is 1.55 GeV (`momentum_unit_GeV_at_Lpar_8_10_16`), coarse against the 10 z-bins.
- Source-free calibration runs (vacuum fake rate b) would multiply the shots by (sqrt(1 + r) + sqrt(r))^2 with r = b / n_s; they are in no printed number.

*Box and lattice.*
- On the 0.8 fm axis the pair recollides after 0.4 fm/c (`recollision_time_fm`), so the 2033 counts describe a box-confined state. The trend uses the box modes 1.55, 3.1 and 4.65 GeV, the last at pa ~ 2.4 (`top_box_mode_times_a` = 2.356); the Lund string wraps the axis 3.9, 7.7 and 11.6 times at full extension (`yoyo_windings_at_full_extension`), so box effects need not cancel in the trend. Separating the pair through 1/Lambda_QCD needs L_par = 20 (2260 LQ, `lq_separation_volume`). The L_par = 4 check in the campaign tests box dependence at one point only.
- The species row runs Sigma(72x3) with H_KS in 2+1D at a ~ 0.1 fm, where freezing is unchecked. Moving it to Sigma(216x3) would need a fermion hop that does not exist and 1092 LQ (`s648_species_lq_if_switched`).
- A second lattice spacing on the same lattice triples the trend; at fixed physical volume it needs 3556 LQ (`lq_second_spacing_fixed_volume`).

*Cost model.*
- The Sigma(72x3) hop rests on an unpublished gate-count draft, with the frame undo, squish uncompute, Toffoli ladders and phasing estimated here; the colour frame is not compiled (section 6). The electric term is a floor until a Sigma(72x3) fast Fourier transform exists.
- The 2033 hop runs one link at a time because its colour-angle flags fill the workspace (F* 7.0-18.5); a streamed-flag schedule would raise F* at a small hop-T cost (a hand estimate in `docs/OPEN_ITEMS.md`, not computed by the model).
- The ramp runs twice per shot at 10^2-10^3 steps each; fixing the ramp at 10^2 steps would cut the campaign's high end substantially (not computed by the model).
- The ~10x cut from a qubitized block encoding of H_KS is assumed (`qubitized_trim`); no Sigma(72x3) block encoding or normalization exists.
- The fault budget counts T gates only.

*Dipole rows.*
- The rows measure <|w|^2> and correct the slope with a Markovian colour model (`floor_slope_ratio`: 0.96 for SU(3), 0.69 for SU(2)). q-hat = 2 GeV^2/fm is taken as the fundamental value; kappa / T^3 = 2.5, P0 = 0.9 and the 1/3 colour factor are assumed.
- The quench-prepared media assume eigenstate thermalization at aT ~ 0.45, checked only on small lattices; the ramp is not tuned to the target temperature, and a coarser thermalization step (2-5x cheaper) is not priced. Pricing the preparation at each row's rule step instead of the 0.01 fm/c grid would raise the static shot to 7.5 x 10^9 - 2.7 x 10^10 T (`t_shot_prep_at_rule_step`, not priced in the headline).
- The static row is 5.4-15x the 10^9 reference. The dipole circuits' T-depth is not analysed.

*Gaps the chapter states.* Bringing the 2033 shot under 10^9 T needs a per-step T-count below about 10^3 at the 2028 scale (a 44x cut, `gap_cut_factor_2028`), a cheaper colour frame and a fast Sigma(72x3) Fourier transform. The campaign's high end needs a 5.2x cut to fit 5 years, the fragmentation instance a 2.3-277x cut.

## 8. Conventions used

The conventions are shared with every chapter and described in the top-level `README.md`: logical qubits count the algorithmic register only; Toffolis at 7 T; every rotation at the full repeat-until-success fit 1.15 log2(1/eps) + 9.2 T with eps set per circuit from its own rotation count so that the total synthesis error is 10^-2 (R-TOL); 0.1 expected faults per shot; wall times on one machine at 1 us per T plus 0.1 ms per shot, corrected by the T-depth when ten factories cannot be fed. The DOE reference budgets (150-250 LQ and 10^5 T in 2028; 1000 LQ and 10^9 T in 2033) are what the chapter calls its limits; the model reports each circuit's ratio to them and does not cap anything: the 2028 shot is 1.32x its reference, the 2033 species shot 4.9-26x and the static dipole shot 5.4-15x the 2033 reference, while the light-like dipole shot fits under it.
